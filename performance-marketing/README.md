# Performance Marketing Specialist

We run paid search, measure what happens after the click, and hand sales a usable next step. The marketing team is small. You would report to Marketing Manager Pavlína. Tools in play: Google Ads, HubSpot, GA4, ClickUp, Cursor.

Group websites: see the [root README](../README.md#public-group-websites).

## What we want to see

Three practical challenges. We care how you think and whether you could run the same process next week — not how pretty one ad looks.

Shop revenue and certified-install leads are different businesses. Mixing them in one campaign, one conversion goal, or one report is the main way to fail this exercise.

## How it works

1. Clone or download this repo (Code → Download ZIP). Work in `performance-marketing/`.
2. **Challenge 01 is mandatory.** Then pick **02 or 03**.
3. Plan about **90 minutes** per challenge.
4. Put work in the `output/` (and `pipeline/` or `system/`) folders for each challenge.
5. Record **one video, 5–10 minutes**, covering both pieces you submit.
6. Email **lucie.kysucanova@airteam.eu**, subject: `Join AIR TEAM — [your name]`.

Send a GitHub repo, a Google Drive folder, or a zip — whatever is easiest. In the email, a link to the work and a link to the video is enough. A polished repo is optional. Prompts and a messy output folder are fine.

If you like CRM more than tags, do **01 + 03**. If you like tracking, **01 + 02**.

## Challenges

| # | Challenge | Required? | What it tests |
|---|-----------|-----------|----------------|
| 01 | [Shop vs upgrade](./01-commerce-vs-leadgen/) | **Yes** | Which traffic is a purchase, which is a quote, and what Google should optimise for |
| 02 | [Measurement spec](./02-measurement-spec/) | Pick one | UTM, Ads, GA4, HubSpot — one event path, two sites |

The three URLs in challenge 01 are fixed. Do not swap them.

## Video

One video, **5–10 minutes**, covering both pieces you submit:

- Why that conversion, and who should own the lead
- Walk through your prompt or script (paste an input, show the output)
- Explain the call, not the tool

## Tools

Use what you already work in: Google Ads Editor, a spreadsheet, Cursor, Claude, ChatGPT, a HubSpot sandbox. We would rather see a repeatable prompt or a small script than three hand-polished headlines.

You do not get our live Ads or HubSpot accounts. Public pages and the files in this repo are enough. Sensitive copy at AIR TEAM is signed off by the Marketing Manager — a note on what you would still send for review is enough.

## What we look for

- Shop ROAS and lead CPL stay in separate buckets.
- Install copy says upgrade / certified install — not “buy a cockpit.”
- Aviation negatives (Bose QC headphones, car GPS, “Garmin watch”) show up when they should.
- The video explains the decision, not the software.
