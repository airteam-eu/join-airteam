# Performance & Growth Marketing Specialist / Lead

This is a take-home, not a CV screen. We do not filter on years of experience; we hire at specialist or lead level. You need an aviation background (you fly, or you have worked in aviation); Czech or Slovak helps. Beyond that, we filter on whether you can keep the businesses apart in ads, measurement and HubSpot, and whether you work with AI as a daily production tool that you check rather than trust.

## The problem

AIR TEAM is an aviation company in Veverská Bítýška, Czech Republic. The public group site is [www.airteam.eu](https://www.airteam.eu/). Two customer-facing businesses must not share one Google Ads goal:

1. **The shop** ([shop.airteam.eu](https://shop.airteam.eu)): boxed avionics and pilot gear. Someone can buy a Bose aviation headset and check out.
2. **The service** ([Avionics upgrades](https://www.airteam.eu/service/avionics/)): in Kunovice we install and certify avionics in the aircraft (EASA Part-145). That is a quote and a project, not an add-to-cart.

If Google treats an install request like a headset purchase, we waste budget and sales get the wrong people.

Live sites for the whole group: [root README](../README.md#public-group-websites).

## The job

Marketing runs as six streams side by side:

| Stream | What it is | Judged on |
|---|---|---|
| Always-on shop | Performance Max, brand search, the Merchant Center feed | shop revenue and ROAS |
| Merchandising | pushing slow stock and clearance through feed labels and listing groups, not extra campaigns | stock that moves |
| Promotions | four a year, each with one publish pack: offer rules, banners, e-mails, ads, posts | revenue and margin of the promotion |
| Content | a monthly flight-safety newsletter, posts, a spring webinar, one source written natively in seven languages | subscribers, clicks, replies; never shop ROAS |
| Leads | Search, forms and HubSpot for avionics upgrades, ELT and headset repairs | cost per lead, leads sales accepts |
| Tender radar | a weekly look at public tenders for avionics and aircraft work | tenders found and answered |

An AI agent in Claude Code drafts most of the material: campaigns, e-mails and posts in seven languages, weekly reports. **You decide what it works on, check everything before it goes out, and turn each correction into a rule in its skills** so the same mistake does not come back.

You work directly with the CEO (priorities, budget, sign-off). A designer handles graphics; technicians send photos from real jobs; sales takes the leads.

**You own:** the campaigns and their goals; the e-mail calendar and every send in HubSpot (segments, waves, a cap on how often anyone hears from us); lead scoring and routing rules (agreed with sales); the agent's briefs and rules; the weekly numbers per stream.

**You do not own:** the websites or tracking implementation (ask IT); closing deals (sales); repair tickets and "it broke" (customer support; those contacts stay out of the sales list).

## We will notice

- Shop ROAS and install CPL never share a campaign goal.
- Install language is upgrade / certified / Part-145 — not "buy a cockpit."
- In aviation PPC, add **negative keywords** for searches that look related but are the wrong customer: consumer Bose headphones (QuietComfort), car GPS, Garmin sports watches, airline cabin-crew jobs.
- A junk contact is ignored, not "nurtured because we have the email."
